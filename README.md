BDTIN Tax Engine AI Pro 🇧🇩
A next-generation Fintech solution designed for the 2026 Bangladesh National Board of Revenue (NBR) tax laws. This tool provides real-time tax calculations, AI-driven tax optimization strategies, and legal loophole identification to help taxpayers manage their assets and returns efficiently.

🚀 Features
Real-time Tax Calculation: Dynamically calculates tax based on updated 2026 NBR slab rates.

AI Optimizer: Analyzes input data to provide personalized "tax loopholes" and legal optimization strategies.

Net Wealth Calculator: Integrated tool to accurately determine net asset value (Assets - Liabilities).

Surcharge Mitigation: Provides intelligent strategies for managing wealth surcharge thresholds.

Modern UI/UX: A sleek, glass-morphism dark-themed interface built with responsive CSS Flexbox and Grid.

Mobile-Optimized: Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

🛠 Tech Stack
HTML5: Semantic structure.

CSS3: Custom glass-morphism, animations, keyframes, and advanced layout techniques.

JavaScript (ES6+): Core tax engine logic, DOM manipulation, and dynamic AI result generation.

Google Fonts: Inter (for clean, professional typography).

💡 How It Works
Input Data: Enter your income sources (Salary, House Property, Agri, etc.) and investment details (DPS, Sanchaypatra, etc.).

Asset Valuation: Use the integrated "Net Wealth Calculator" to factor in land, flats, bank balances, and liabilities.

Run Engine: Click "হিসাব ও এক্সপার্ট বিশ্লেষণ করুন" (Calculate & Expert Analysis).

AI Analysis: The engine processes your data against 2026 NBR laws and outputs actionable advice in the AI Panel.

📈 Tax Engine Logic
The engine adheres to:

Progressive slab-based tax calculation.

Standard deductions for salary and rental income.

Investment tax rebates (up to 30% or 1.5 Crore limit).

New minimum tax guidelines (2026).
